Frequently asked questions

Answers to the most common questions about using Diyaa

Questions and answers

Are my photos uploaded to the internet?

Never. All processing happens inside your browser, on your own device. No file is ever sent to a server. Your photos stay 100% private and never leave your device.

Is Diyaa really free?

Yes, completely. No account, no subscription, and no usage restrictions. We believe essential tools should be available to everyone.

Which formats are supported?

We support JPG, PNG, WebP, AVIF, HEIC, TIFF, GIF, BMP, and SVG. You can convert between these formats and combine images into a single PDF.

Is there a file size limit?

The recommended maximum is 50MB per file, and 50 images per batch, for smooth performance on most devices.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. The site is fully responsive and works on any modern browser — mobile, desktop, or tablet. You can also install it as a web app (PWA).

Do I need an internet connection?

Only to load the site initially. Once it's loaded, you can keep using it even offline (if installed as a PWA) — processing itself always happens locally.

Are my images stored on the site?

No. We don't operate servers that store images. Everything happens in your browser's temporary memory and is cleared when you close the page or clear your files.

Can I use it commercially?

Yes. You can use Diyaa for personal and commercial purposes without restriction.
