Privacy Policy

Your privacy is the foundation of what we build. We don't collect your data, and we don't upload your photos.

🔒 Our core principle

At Diyaa, we believe your privacy is a non-negotiable, fundamental right. That's why we built our platform to run entirely inside your browser, with no need to upload any file to our servers.

📋 What we don't do

  • ❌ We don't upload your photos — all processing happens locally on your device
  • ❌ We don't store your data — there is no database on our servers
  • ❌ We don't track you — we don't use Google Analytics or any tracking tools
  • ❌ We don't sell your data — we have nothing to sell in the first place
  • ❌ We don't use tracking cookies — none are set for tracking purposes

✅ What we do

  • ✅ Local processing — everything happens inside your own browser
  • ✅ Temporary memory only — images are held only in your browser's own memory while you use the tool
  • ✅ Automatic clearing — closing the page or clearing your files removes all data instantly

🌐 Hosting

Diyaa is served as static files (HTML, CSS, and JavaScript) from standard static web hosting. Static hosting has no ability to see or access any data that's processed inside your browser — the files are simply downloaded to your device and run there.
