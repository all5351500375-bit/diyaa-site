LinguaGo — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Who we are

LinguaGo ("we") develops the LinguaGo app (Android package com.linguagoapp). Contact: aall0506641401@gmail.com.

Summary

LinguaGo does not require an account. We do not run servers that receive your learning data, and the app contains no analytics or crash-reporting tools. Your learning data stays on your device. Some lessons can be unlocked by watching an optional rewarded ad provided by Google AdMob; that ad service is described below.

Data stored on your device

The app stores on your device only: your settings (app language, theme, playback speed, whether to show sentence translations), your lesson progress, the words you save, and which lessons you unlocked. This data is not sent to us. You can delete progress and saved words in Settings; uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

Lesson videos

Lesson videos and texts are included in the app. Playing a lesson does not require an internet connection.

Pronunciation

Word pronunciation uses the text-to-speech engine installed on your device. The word is passed to that engine on your device; the app does not send it to us. Whether it works offline depends on the voices installed on your device.

Dictionary

The English-Arabic dictionary is included in the app and works offline. Searching it sends nothing to us.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app includes the Google Mobile Ads SDK to show optional rewarded ads that unlock some lessons. The SDK is started only when you open a lesson page, and an ad is shown only when you choose to watch one. When it is active, Google may collect and process data such as device identifiers (including the advertising ID), IP address and the approximate location derived from it, device and app information (for example model, operating system, language and app version), and ad interaction, performance and diagnostic data. Google uses this data to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud and, where permitted, personalise ads. We receive only aggregated reports from AdMob, not data that identifies you. See how Google uses data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Consent and ad choices

Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), Google's consent message is shown before ads are requested, and you can change your choice later from "Ad privacy choices" in Settings when that option is available. Ad content is limited to the general-audience rating (G). You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android device settings.

Permissions

The app itself requests no special permissions. The Google Mobile Ads SDK adds internet access, network state and advertising ID permissions so that ads can load. The app does not use your camera, microphone, contacts or precise location.

Children

LinguaGo is an educational app suitable for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and have a concern, contact us.

Sharing

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share your learning data. Advertising data is handled by Google as described above.

Security

Data on your device is protected by the standard Android app sandbox. We do not add separate encryption to this local data.

Your choices

Ads are optional: free lessons never require watching an ad. You can clear progress and saved words in Settings, uninstall the app at any time, and contact us with any privacy question or request.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, we will update this policy and its date before the change takes effect.
