Frequently asked questions about hEIC to JPG Converter

Why are my iPhone photos in HEIC format?

Apple uses HEIC by default to save storage space, since it compresses photos more efficiently than JPG at similar quality.

Which browsers can convert HEIC here?

Safari can decode HEIC natively, so this tool works out of the box there. Other browsers (Chrome, Firefox, Edge) currently can't decode HEIC without an external library, which this fully offline tool intentionally avoids — in that case, please convert the photo to JPG first using your iPhone or computer's built-in Share/Export option, then use that JPG file.

Does this tool upload my photos?

No — whenever conversion is possible, it happens entirely inside your browser, and your photos never touch a server.

Can I convert Live Photos?

This tool converts the still image; the Live Photo's video component isn't included.
