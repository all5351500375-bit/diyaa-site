Frequently asked questions about sharpen Image Online

How does sharpening work?

It applies a convolution kernel that increases contrast at edges, making detail appear crisper without changing the overall image size.

Can sharpening fix a very blurry photo?

Sharpening enhances existing edges but can't recover detail that was never captured — it works best on photos that are only slightly soft.

What amount should I start with?

1.0 is a moderate default; increase gradually and check the result, since very high values can introduce visible haloing around edges.

Is this processed locally?

Yes, entirely in your browser via pixel-level canvas processing — no uploads.
