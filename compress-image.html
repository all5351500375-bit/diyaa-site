Frequently asked questions about compress Image Online

Will I lose visible quality?

At 80% compression, the difference is usually imperceptible to the eye for most photos — you can always raise the quality slider if you notice artifacts.

What's the best output format for compression?

WebP generally gives the smallest file size at a given quality; JPG is the safest universally-compatible choice for photos; PNG is best only when you need lossless quality or transparency.

Can I compress many images at once?

Yes, batch-process as many images as you like and download them all as a ZIP file.

Is my photo uploaded to be compressed?

No — compression happens entirely in your browser.
