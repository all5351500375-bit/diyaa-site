About Diyaa

We believe privacy is a basic right, and that digital tools should serve you — not exploit you.

🎯 Our mission

Diyaa is a dedicated image conversion and editing platform, built from the ground up so your photos never leave your device. Every operation runs locally in your browser using modern web technology. No servers, no uploads, no tracking — just powerful, safe, fast tools.

🔧 How it works

Diyaa uses standard, modern web technology:

  • Canvas API: for efficiently drawing and processing images
  • File API: for reading files from your device locally
  • Blob API: for creating and downloading the processed files
  • Canvas filter pipeline: for adjustments like blur, saturation, and hue, without freezing the interface

When you choose an image, it's read as a Blob straight into your browser's own memory. It's then processed with JavaScript and the Canvas API, and the result is saved to your device. Not a single byte of your image data is ever sent over the internet.

🛡️ Security & privacy

Diyaa runs with no backend servers at all. Everything happens in your browser:

  • ✓ No databases store your photos
  • ✓ No logs are kept of the files you process
  • ✓ Your activity is never tracked, and no data is collected about you
  • ✓ No tracking cookies
  • ✓ The site keeps working even offline, once it's been loaded the first time
