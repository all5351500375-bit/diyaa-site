Frequently asked questions

Are my photos uploaded to a server?

No. Every conversion, edit, and export runs entirely inside your browser using the Canvas API and Web APIs. Your files never leave your device.

Which formats can I convert between?

You can convert JPG, PNG, WebP, AVIF, HEIC, TIFF, GIF, BMP, and SVG into PNG, JPG, or ICO, and combine any images into a single PDF.

Is there a limit on how many images I can process?

There's no artificial limit built into the tools — performance simply depends on your device's own memory and processing power. For very large batches, we recommend working in smaller groups for the smoothest experience.

Is Diyaa really free?

Yes, completely free with no account, subscription, or watermark. We believe essential everyday tools should be available to everyone.

Can I use Diyaa on my phone?

Yes — the site is fully responsive and works in any modern mobile browser, with no app to install.

Does Diyaa support HEIC and AVIF?

Yes, both are supported for conversion. HEIC decoding depends on your browser: it works natively in Safari, while other browsers may need the photo pre-converted to JPG first.
